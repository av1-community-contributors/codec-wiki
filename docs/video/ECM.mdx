# ECM

ECM (Enhanced Compression Model) is not a finished codec but a **research test model** by JVET (the ISO/IEC MPEG + ITU-T VCEG joint team behind [VVC](./VVC.mdx)): a fork of the [VTM](../encoders/VTM.mdx) reference encoder stacked with experimental coding tools that go beyond what the VVC standard allows. Its purpose is to answer one question, *how much more compression can still be squeezed out of the traditional block-based video coding architecture?*, with computational cost as a secondary concern.

The results of that exploration are significant: ECM-20.0 reports roughly **28% average luma bitrate reduction versus VTM** on natural content in random-access coding, and around **43% on screen content** (per JVET reporting; individual videos vary). On an informal like-for-like test (same tiny clip, same QP), ECM-20.0 encoded ~24% smaller than VTM 24.0 at slightly higher PSNR, while taking roughly 10× longer to do it.

With ECM 20.0 (2026), JVET considers the exploration phase complete & has issued a joint Call for Proposals for video coding beyond VVC (July 2026, responses to be evaluated in January 2027 & a first standard version targeted for October 2029), from which a future standard (informally "H.267") will be selected. ECM is therefore best understood as the bridge between VVC and that next standard, not as a deliverable codec itself.

## What's actually in it

ECM keeps VVC's overall architecture (block-based hybrid coding, CTU partitioning, in-loop filtering) & layers dozens of exploratory tools on top. The headline themes:

- **Smarter prediction without signaling**: decoder-side intra mode derivation (DIMD/TIMD), template matching prediction (IntraTMP), & cross-component prediction improvements (CCCM variants) let the decoder *derive* what VVC would have to spend bits signaling.
- **Heavier inter prediction**: local illumination compensation, template-matching-based merge candidate reordering, multi-pass DMVR/BDOF refinement, geometric partitioning (GPM) extensions, & sub-pel intra block copy for screen content.
- **Bigger & more flexible transforms**: CTU/transform sizes up to 256×256, 8-state dependent quantization (VVC: 4), non-separable primary transforms (NSPT), & expanded transform selection.
- **More in-loop filtering**: larger adaptive loop filters, temporal ALF, a bilateral filter (BIF), cross-component SAO, & optionally a neural-network loop filter (NNLF, requires a build with the NN extension enabled).
- **Entropy & precision**: 15-bit CABAC probability precision, 12-bit internal pipeline, & better coefficient sign/level prediction.

The full tool inventory is documented in the ECM algorithm description documents in the [ECM repository](https://vcgit.hhi.fraunhofer.de/ecm/ECM); the above is what matters for understanding its performance profile.

## Practical reality

- **Nothing decodes ECM but ECM.** The bitstream is intentionally *not* VVC-compatible: [FFmpeg](../utilities/ffmpeg.mdx)'s VVC decoder rejects it outright, no hardware decodes it, & no player supports it. Round-tripping requires the reference `DecoderApp`.
- **Encoding is extraordinarily slow.** The encoder is single-threaded, like VTM, but 5-10× slower still; expect hours per second of 1080p video on a single core.
- **It is reference-grade software**: configuration-file driven, fixed-QP or simple bitrate-targeting rate control, no scene detection, raw YUV/Y4M input only.

Unless you are doing codec research or benchmarking future-standard candidates, there is no practical reason to encode with ECM today; see the [ECM encoder entry](../encoders/ECM.mdx) if you do.
